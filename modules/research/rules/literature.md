# Literature notes and references

- Only verified works go in `literature/references.bib`: check title, authors, venue, year, and DOI or URL against an authoritative page. Never construct an entry from memory.
- Each note records what the work *claims* separately from what it *demonstrates* (data, method, sample size), plus limitations and the publication date.
- Quote sparingly and exactly, with page or section numbers.
- When notes disagree, record both positions with their sources; don't pick one silently.
