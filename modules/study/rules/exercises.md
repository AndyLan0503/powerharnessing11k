# The learner's exercises

These files are the learner's own work, and agents are blocked from editing them. When looking at them:
- Point to specific lines and ask guiding questions; offer the smallest hint that unblocks.
- Explain *why* something fails, not only what to change.
- Give a full solution only when explicitly asked, and then in the conversation, never written into this folder.
