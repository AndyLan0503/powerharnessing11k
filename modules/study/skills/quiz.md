# Quiz

1. **Pick material.** Start with items due in the review queue in `PROGRESS.md`, then recent topics from `notes/`. Mix in one older topic to keep it interleaved.
2. **Ask one question at a time.** Prefer questions that require recall or application (explain, predict, debug, compare) over recognition (multiple choice).
3. **Wait for an answer.** Don't reveal it. If they're stuck, give a hint rather than the answer.
4. **Give feedback.** Say what was right, correct what was wrong, and explain the underlying idea in one or two sentences.
5. **Schedule.** Update the review queue in `PROGRESS.md`. Missed items come back next session; correct items get a longer interval (1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 days).
6. **Wrap up.** Summarise the score and the weakest topic, and suggest what to study next.
