# Study planning

1. **Goal.** What should they be able to *do* at the end, and by when? Make it concrete and testable (e.g. "build X", "pass Y", "explain Z without notes").
2. **Inventory.** Current level, available hours per week, and preferred resources (book, course, docs).
3. **Syllabus.** Break the goal into ordered topics, each with: a resource, an exercise to prove it, and an estimated time. Put prerequisites first.
4. **This week.** Pick 3 to 5 sessions with a specific topic and exercise each. Leave slack.
5. **Adjust.** Compare `PROGRESS.md` with the plan. If behind, cut scope or extend the date; don't cram. If ahead, add depth or a project.
6. Write the result to `LEARNING_PLAN.md` and confirm it with the learner before saving.
