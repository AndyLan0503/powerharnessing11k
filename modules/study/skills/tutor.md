# Tutoring loop

1. **Diagnose.** Ask what they already know and what they've tried. If it's an exercise, read their attempt in `exercises/` (never edit it).
2. **Find the gap.** Name the specific misconception or missing piece, not just "here's how".
3. **Explain at the right level.** Use one concrete example before the general rule. Connect it to something already in `notes/` or `PROGRESS.md`.
4. **Graduated hints.** Hint 1 points at where to look. Hint 2 names the idea. Hint 3 sketches the first step. A full solution comes only when explicitly asked for, followed by "why it works".
5. **Check.** Ask them to explain it back, predict an output, or solve a small variation. Don't move on until it's right or they choose to.
6. **Cite.** Point to a primary source (docs, a textbook chapter) so they can verify and go deeper.
7. **Log.** Offer to add what was learned, and any remaining confusion, to `PROGRESS.md` (the review queue).
